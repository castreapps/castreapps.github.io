# Nox privacy policy

Effective 6 November 2026. [Leer en español](privacy-policy.es.md)

Nox is an audiobook player for books you already have on your phone. It has no account, no analytics and no ads,
and the developer receives none of your data. This page describes what Nox keeps on the phone and the few cases
where something leaves it.

## What Nox keeps on your phone

- **Your library**: the folders you link, and for each book details such as its title, author, narrator, chapters
  and duration, and a copy of its cover. Nox reads this from the files themselves.
- **Your listening**: where you are in each book, when you last listened, and the speed you chose for it.
- **Your marks**: the moments you save, with any title, note or quote you write.
- **Your settings**: preferences such as the rewind length and the app's language.

Nox only reads the folders you pick through Android's folder picker. It doesn't search the rest of your storage.
Uninstalling Nox, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes all of this.

## When something leaves your phone

### Android backup

If backup is turned on for your phone, Android saves your library, listening, marks and settings to your own Google
account, and restores them when you set up a new phone. The audio files and covers aren't included, and neither is
Nox's access to your folders: after a restore you link the folders again. Google handles this backup under its own
[privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can turn it off in your phone's backup settings.

### Casting to a speaker or TV

Casting uses Google Cast, which is part of Google Play services.

- To show whether a Cast device is available, Nox looks for Cast devices on your local network while the player
  screen is open.
- While you cast a book, Nox serves that book's audio files and cover to your Cast device over your local network.
  Each file is offered under a random address that only works during that Cast session, and nothing is sent outside
  your network.
- Google's Cast software logs how it finds and connects to Cast devices, along with information about the phone and
  the app, and sends part of this to Google. Google
  [describes this data](https://developers.google.com/cast/docs/android_sender/data_disclosure) as not tied to a
  person and kept for a short time. Neither the developer nor you can turn this logging off.

Apart from Cast and the Google Play features below, Nox doesn't use the network.

### Google Play features

If you installed Nox from Google Play, Nox may use two Google Play features:

- **Update checks**: Nox may ask Google Play whether a newer version is available. Google Play uses your device
  details and the installed version to answer.
- **Ratings**: Nox may show Google Play's rating card. Anything you write there is a Google Play review, posted
  publicly under your Google Play profile, and you can edit or delete it from Google Play.

Google runs both features under its [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The developer only sees
your review as Google Play shows it to every developer, and doesn't receive your device details.

## Children

Nox isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly handle children's personal data.
