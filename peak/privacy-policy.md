# Peak privacy policy

Effective 16 November 2026. [Leer en español](privacy-policy.es.md)

Peak is a fitness app for planning and logging your gym workouts, and for seeing the activity, sleep and body
measurements already on your phone. It has no account, no analytics and no ads, and it doesn't use the internet: the
developer receives none of your data. This page describes what Peak keeps on your phone, what it reads from Health
Connect, and the few cases where something leaves the phone.

## What Peak keeps on your phone

- **Your training**: the exercises you create (with their equipment and muscles), your routines, and every workout
  you log, with its sets, weights, reps and times.
- **Figures worked out from Health Connect**: so that lists open quickly, Peak stores the distance, average heart
  rate and calories of each activity session, and the average heart rate of each night's sleep. Nothing else read
  from Health Connect is stored.
- **Your settings**: preferences such as the range shown on the Body chart.

Uninstalling Peak, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes all of this.

## Health Connect

Peak reads data from Health Connect, Android's on-device store for health and fitness data, only after you allow it
in Health Connect's permission screen. Each permission can be granted or removed at any time in Health Connect's
settings. Peak never writes to Health Connect.

| Data Peak reads | What it's for |
|---|---|
| Exercise sessions, with distance, heart rate, calories burned, steps, elevation gained and VO2 max | The Activity tab and each session's detail; matching a gym session recorded by your watch with the workout you logged in Peak |
| Exercise routes | The map of a run or ride, shown only when you open that session and only with your consent for it |
| Steps, calories burned and floors climbed | Today's activity and your daily average |
| Sleep, resting heart rate, heart rate variability and breathing rate | The Sleep list and each night's detail |
| Weight, body fat, lean body mass, bone mass and body water mass | The Body tab and its chart |
| Data from before you installed Peak (past data) | Showing your full history rather than only the last 30 days |

Peak reads this data when a screen needs it and shows it to you, on your phone. Apart from the figures listed above,
it doesn't keep a copy. Peak doesn't sell, share or transfer Health Connect data to anyone, and doesn't use it for
advertising. Nobody, the developer included, can see it.

Peak's use of information received from Health Connect will adhere to the Health Connect Permissions policy,
including the Limited Use requirements.

## When something leaves your phone

### Your watch

If you use Peak's Wear OS app, the phone sends your routines and the workout in progress to the watch, and the watch
sends back the sets you log and the changes you make. They travel directly between the two devices through Google
Play services' Wear OS connection, only while the watch is nearby. The watch shows this data while you train and
keeps none of it.

### Update checks

Peak may ask Google Play whether a newer version of Peak is available, and offer it with Google Play's own
dialog. To answer, Google Play uses your device's details and the installed version of Peak, as it does when it
updates your apps.

### Moving to a new phone

Peak's data is left out of Android's cloud backup (Google Drive), since workouts are health data. When you set up a
new phone by transferring data directly from the old one (with a cable or over Wi-Fi), Android copies Peak's data
along. That transfer happens between your two phones.

Apart from these, nothing leaves your phone.

## Children

Peak isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly handle children's personal data.
